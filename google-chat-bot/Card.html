NEON PONG

Supercharged 2-player pong with tricks, targets, and smashes

🎮 How to Play

Drag your paddle. Hit gold targets, build rallies (x2 at 5, x3 at 10, x4 at 20), land edge, spin and smash tricks, and race to 5 goals!

🌐 Online Multiplayer

Play with a friend! Share a room code and connect via WebSocket.

🤖 Single Player

Challenge the AI at 3 difficulty levels.
